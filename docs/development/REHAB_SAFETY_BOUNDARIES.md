# Rehab safety boundaries

## What this product is

A personal tracker for clinician-guided rehab plans and performed sessions.

## Required user-facing language

- This is a tracking tool, not medical advice.
- Follow the instructions of a qualified clinician.
- Stop and seek professional guidance for sharp pain, sudden swelling,
  instability, locking, faintness, or unusual symptoms.

## Must not

- Tell the user an injury is safe
- Recommend ignoring pain
- Automatically increase load, range, volume, or difficulty
- Infer clinical clearance or return-to-sport
- Replace clinician restrictions with app logic
- Merge general workout progression into rehab
- Diagnose, triage emergencies, or act as a clinician portal

Every automated-looking action remains user-confirmed. Alert continue requires
explicit acknowledgment and does not claim continuing is safe.
